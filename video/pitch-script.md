# Vesta pitch script (about 1:50)

Read it in your own voice. Each part goes with one slide in `video/pitch.py`.

**1. Team**
Hi, I'm angelraph, and this is Vesta. I'm a solo builder: I did the product, the design and the engineering. I built it for people I know: housemates in the UK who pay rent together and support family back home.

**2. Problem**
Today one house runs on four apps. A banking app for rent. A group chat to chase who owes what. Mental maths for every bill. And a remittance app that charges a fee, takes a cut on the rate, and still takes days. Someone always ends up chasing.

**3. Product**
Vesta is the bank account for a household. You open it with your phone lock: no password, no seed phrase. Everyone pays rent into one pot, bills split with a tap, and money home lands in seconds with a receipt your family can open. The blockchain stays invisible.

**4. Why now**
This is only possible now. Monad settles every payment in about a second. Mera makes one passkey the whole account. Agora settles money home instantly and lets family convert it back. Chainlink runs rent day on its own, and Envio keeps the household ledger.

**5. Traction**
And people are using it. This week, eight friends moved into one real house on Vesta, seven of them through an invite link. All eight paid their rent share, and six of them sent money home. One tester said: sign in is sharp, sharp, and smooth.

**6. Close**
Houses grow by invitation, so every sign-up brings the housemates with it. Vesta: money for the people you live with. Try it now on your phone.
